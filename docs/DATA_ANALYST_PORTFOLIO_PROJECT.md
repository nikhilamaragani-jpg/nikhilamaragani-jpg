# Retail Performance & Customer Insights Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project was designed as a portfolio-ready Data Analyst case study to show core business analysis skills. It focuses on understanding how a retail business performs across time, regions, customer segments, and sales channels.

## Business Question

The goal is to answer practical business questions such as:
- Which months and periods drive the most revenue?
- Which regions generate the strongest sales?
- Which sales channels perform best?
- Which product categories drive the highest value?
- Which actions can improve profit and customer retention?

## Dataset and Scope

The project uses a synthetic retail dataset with over 1,500 orders, covering:
- order date
- region
- sales channel
- category
- customer segment
- quantity
- unit price
- discount
- revenue
- profit
- order status

## Process

### 1. Data Preparation
- raw records were generated in a structured retail format
- missing and inconsistent elements were handled during analysis readiness
- columns were standardized for reporting and KPIs

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis
- monthly revenue trend analysis
- regional sales comparison
- category revenue and quantity analysis
- channel performance comparison
- order status and business risk review

### 3. KPI Reporting
The project calculated key metrics including:
- total revenue
- total profit
- average order value
- total orders
- profit margin
- returned orders rate

### 4. Visualization and Dashboarding
The final output includes a dashboard banner that communicates insights visually through:
- monthly revenue trend
- revenue by region
- revenue by category
- revenue by sales channel

## Key Insights

- revenue clusters around a strong seasonal pattern with a significant peak toward the end of the year
- online and marketplace channels show stronger sales potential than brick-and-mortar retail
- the West and North regions are the strongest revenue contributors
- Electronics and Home categories remain the most valuable segments
- improving retention strategy and promotional efficiency can improve longer-term profitability

## Recommendations

1. Focus customer retention campaigns on underperforming regions.
2. Use premium bundles and upsell strategies in high-value categories.
3. Rebalance discounting to avoid margin erosion in low-volume products.
4. Allocate more inventory to the strongest regions and highest-converting channels.
5. Create a recurring dashboard for stakeholders to monitor trends over time.
