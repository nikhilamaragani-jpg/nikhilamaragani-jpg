## About

I’m an early-career Data Analyst and 2026 B.Tech Computer Science Engineering graduate focused on turning raw data into reliable analysis, dashboards, and actionable business insights.

My core analytics stack is SQL, Python/Pandas, Power BI, Power Query, DAX, data visualization, data quality, and business analysis. I build reproducible projects that move from business questions through data preparation, metric definition, analysis, visualization, interpretation, and recommendations.

My current flagship project is **E-Commerce Operations & Customer Intelligence**, a real-data case study built from the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset.

The project covers:
- Revenue and sales performance
- Customer and RFM analysis
- Product/category analysis
- Delivery and logistics performance
- Customer review experience
- Geographic performance
- SQL analysis and Python/Pandas
- Power BI semantic-model and DAX design
- Interactive slicers, cross-filtering, metric switching and drill-through-style analysis
- Dashboard storytelling, tooltips, conditional formatting, and QA

Verified project results include R$13.49M merchandise revenue across 98,199 sales-eligible orders, 94,983 unique customers, a 3.04% observed repeat-customer rate, 93.23% on-time delivery, and an average review score of 4.07/5. These are historical Olist dataset results, not current market statistics.

The project also shows how I think as an analyst: top five categories account for about 39.8% of revenue, the top three states account for about 63.4%, and late deliveries are associated with materially lower observed review scores. I explicitly separate observations from recommendations and avoid causal claims the data cannot support.
